Expectancy Theory adds another important perspective by suggesting that limited training, recurring software issues, and changing bonus criteria may have reduced employees' confidence that their effort would lead to successful performance and meaningful rewards. Together, these theories explain different aspects of the motivation problem observed at Riverview.
