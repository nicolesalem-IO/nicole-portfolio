Crisp workplace wellness needs assessment survey

This survey is designed to gain a deeper understanding of the wellness needs and experiences of Crisp employees. Your feedback will help us assess how recent changes, including our wellness policies and workforce growth, are impacting employees and how we can better support their overall well-being. The results will inform the development of future programs and resources that promote health, engagement, and job satisfaction. All responses are voluntary and anonymous. The survey is open to all Crisp employees, excluding executive leadership.

Section 1: Overall Well-Being & Engagement

Rated on a five-point scale: Strongly Disagree · Disagree · Neutral · Agree · Strongly Agree.

  1. Crisp supports a healthy balance between my work and personal life.
  2. I feel a sense of belonging and connection with my colleagues at Crisp.
  3. Employee input is considered before job-related decisions are made.
  4. I have enough control over how I organize and complete my work tasks.
  5. Employees in my work group treat one another with respect.
